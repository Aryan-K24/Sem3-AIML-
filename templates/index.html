📐 Mathematical Formulation of Rating Deflator

The genuine customer rating $R_{authentic}$ eliminates artificial promotional distortion by computing a Bayesian-adjusted weighted organic expectation:

R_authentic = ( ∑ (1 - P(fake_i)) * r_i ) / ∑ (1 - P(fake_i))

Where $P(fake_i)$ is the posterior fraud probability predicted by our hybrid Logistic-TF-IDF forensic classifier, and $r_i \in [1, 5]$ is the customer star rating.
